**e‑Voter (e‑Voting) Registration Process**

The provided document explains that e‑voter registration depends on whether a shareholder is already registered with NSDL/CDSL facilities.

---

### **1. Shareholders Registered on Easi / Easiest Facility**
- Use existing Demat account login credentials provided by the Depository Participant (DP).
- Log in with **User ID, Password, and Verification Code** on the NSDL/CDSL website.
- After successful login, click on **“e‑vote”** in the action tab to access the e‑voting system [test.pdf, Page 4, Chunk chunk-6, "Shareholders registered on Easi/Easiest facility"]

---

### **2. Shareholders Not Registered on Easi / Easiest Facility**
- Visit the NSDL/CDSL e‑voting website.
- Click on the registration option **“Easi/Easiest”**.
- Enter Demat account details, registered email ID, and mobile number.
- Generate and enter the **OTP** received on the registered email ID and mobile number.
- After registration, follow the same login steps as registered Easi/Easiest users [test.pdf, Page 4, Chunk chunk-6, "Shareholders not registered on Easi/Easiest facility"]

---

### **3. Shareholders Not Registered on IDeAS Facility**
- Visit the e‑voting website.
- Enter BO ID / Demat Account Number and PAN.
- Complete OTP‑based authentication.
- Click on the **e‑voting option**, which redirects to the e‑voting page [test.pdf, Page 4, Chunk chunk-6, "Shareholders not registered on IDeAS facility"]

---

### **4. Registration / Access Through NSDL e‑Voting Website**
- Visit **https://www.evoting.nsdl.com**.
- Click on **“Login”** under the *Shareholder/Member* section.
- Enter Demat details and complete OTP authentication.
- Upon successful login, the e‑voting option becomes available [test.pdf, Page 65, Chunk chunk-9, "Quick links to access e-voting system"]

---
