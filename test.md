## Additional Notes and References for **“Purchase of Treasury Shares”** (Easy‑to‑Understand Explanation)\n\n### What does “Purchase of treasury shares” mean?\nWhen Nestlé **buys back its own shares from the market**, those shares are called **treasury shares**. These shares are not counted as outstanding shares and **do not receive dividends**.\n\n---\n\n### Key notes and references\nThe purchase of treasury shares is mainly explained in:\n- **Note 16 – Treasury shares**\n- **Note 17.4 – Number of shares outstanding**\n- **Note 15 – Changes in equity**\n\nThese notes describe **how many shares were bought**, **why they were bought**, and **how they affected share capital and equity**. cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-170chunk-264chunk-278\n\n---\n\n### What happened in 2024? (Relevant data)\n\n#### 1. Shares bought back\n- Nestlé purchased **48.8 million shares** under its **share buyback program**.\n- The total cost of these purchases was **CHF 4.4 billion**.\n- All treasury shares are recorded at their **purchase (acquisition) cost**. cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-278\n\n#### 2. Use of treasury shares\nTreasury shares held at **December 31, 2024**:\n- **43.5 million shares** from the **share buyback program**\n- **3.8 million shares** held to cover **long‑term incentive plans**\n- **Total treasury shares:** **47.3 million shares** cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-170chunk-264\n\n#### 3. Share cancellation\n- Nestlé **cancelled 50.0 million shares** during 2024.\n- This reduced share capital from **CHF 267 million to CHF 262 million**.\n- The cancelled shares had a purchase value of **CHF 5.3 billion**. cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-278\n\n#### 4. Effect on shares outstanding\n- Outstanding shares decreased by **50.2 million** due to purchases.\n- **1.6 million shares** were reissued for employee equity compensation.\n- Final outstanding shares at **December 31, 2024:** **2,572.7 million shares**. cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-170\n\n---\n\n### Overall impact\n- Treasury shares represented **1.8% of Nestlé’s share capital** at year‑end 2024 (unchanged from 2023).\n- Their **market value** was **CHF 3.5 billion** at December 31, 2024.\n- Buybacks and cancellations reduced the number of shares available to investors, which can **increase earnings per share**. cite2024-financial-statements-en.pdfchunk-170
